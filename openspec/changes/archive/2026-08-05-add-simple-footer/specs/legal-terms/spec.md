## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Accessible Legal Terms Page
The system SHALL provide a dedicated `/terminos` page detailing the terms of service and privacy practices of Quempo.

#### Scenario: User navigates to /terminos
- **WHEN** a user visits `/terminos`
- **THEN** the system displays a clear, readable document outlining Quempo's P2P neutrality, user responsibility, data privacy, and community guidelines with a link to return to the home page
